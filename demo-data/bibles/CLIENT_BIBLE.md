# Purpose

The Client Bible defines every client used within the Orion MVP.

Each client has its own business profile, payment behaviour,
relationship history and financial characteristics.

Invoices, reminders, payments, dashboard insights and AI
recommendations MUST be generated using these client profiles.

# Client Behaviour Profiles

Every client belongs to one of these payment personas.

## Premium

- Pays before due date
- Never needs reminders
- High trust score

---

## Reliable

- Pays within payment terms
- Occasionally needs one reminder

---

## Delayed

- Pays 10–15 days after due date
- Usually replies to reminders

---

## Slow

- Requires multiple reminders
- Finance approval delays

---

## Enterprise

- High invoice values
- Net 45 terms
- Long approval workflows

---

## High Risk

- Frequently overdue
- Partial payments
- Escalation required

# Business Rules

No client changes payment behaviour randomly.

Payment patterns remain consistent across months.

Enterprise clients generate fewer but larger invoices.

Educational institutions generate invoices around semester
and placement seasons.

Training partners generate recurring monthly invoices.

Government clients have the longest payment cycles.

---

# AI Rules

Orion continuously learns from each client's payment behaviour.

Every successful payment increases trust.

Repeated late payments decrease trust.

Promise-to-pay commitments increase confidence if honoured.

Customer intelligence is recalculated after every payment.
